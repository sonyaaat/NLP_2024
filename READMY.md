# NLP Laboratory Works

This repository contains laboratory assignments for an NLP (Natural Language Processing) course. The works focus on various aspects of NLP, including neural networks, word embeddings, and text classification. Below is an overview of the tasks included in the repository:

## RNN Quest
In this task, we explore Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs) by training a model on a simple sequence-to-sequence problem. The goal is to train a language model, overfitting it on a specific message. After training, the model's weights are saved for further evaluation.

## Spellchecker Quest
This task involves building a spellchecker using n-gram models and evaluating its performance on text corrections. The goal is to generate the most probable corrections based on n-grams learned from a training corpus.

## Workbook 02: Exploring Word Embeddings
In this workbook, we dive deeper into word embeddings. We:
- Load pre-trained GloVe vectors.
- Explore interesting properties of these word embeddings, such as word similarity and analogy tasks.

The objective is to understand how word vectors capture semantic meaning and relationships between words.

## Workbook 03: Word Embeddings for Text Classification
This workbook demonstrates how to use word embeddings to train a text classification model. 
The focus is on how word embeddings can be particularly useful when training data is scarce, which is often the case in real-world scenarios.
